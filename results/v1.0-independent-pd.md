# v1.0 — Independent PD

[简体中文](v1.0-independent-pd.zh-CN.md)

## Objective

Use the RTX 3060 as an independent prefill endpoint and the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 / Radeon 8060S as the state-owning decode endpoint, then measure the impact of heterogeneous state handoff on TTFT, prefill, and decode.

## Measurement boundary

- One host with RTX 3060 12GB and Radeon 8060S
- 9B Q6_K
- 5064 input tokens and 128 output tokens
- Server-request measurement with cache disabled; second result retained

## Nodes

| Node | RTX 3060 scope | TTFT | Prefill | Decode | State handoff |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| AI Max+ 395 baseline | 0 tokens | 5.879 s | 861.55 tok/s | 30.24 tok/s | None |
| PD-512 | 512 tokens | 5.720 s | 886.62 tok/s | 30.22 tok/s | 512 tokens |
| PD-2048 | 2048 tokens | 5.077 s | 999.16 tok/s | 30.18 tok/s | 2048 tokens |
| Full PD | Full prompt | 3.496 s | 1452.29 tok/s | 30.28 tok/s | 5063 tokens / 208.57 MiB |

## Observation

Full independent PD was the best v1.0 node. Relative to the AI Max+ 395 baseline, TTFT fell by 2.383 s and prefill rose by 590.74 tok/s while decode remained effectively unchanged. The partial nodes show increasing benefit with a larger handoff scope, but the result must not be extrapolated to untested models or long contexts.

## Positioning

Independent PD also preserves the architectural potential for concurrent prefill and decode across requests. v1.0 did not quantify that potential under concurrent load.
